# Concepts

Shared domain vocabulary for this project — entities, named processes, and status concepts with project-specific meaning. Seeded with core domain vocabulary, then accretes as ce-compound and ce-compound-refresh process learnings; direct edits are fine. Glossary only, not a spec or catch-all.

### Jokbo
A family genealogy that records people and lineage relationships, often in a chart.

### Hojok
A historical household register used to record households for government administration.

### Hogu danja
A household declaration submitted for household registration.

### Junhogu
An official document certifying household register information.

### Person appearance
A reference to a possible person in a particular source passage, which may or may not identify the same individual as another appearance.

### Person candidate
An unreviewed proposed person appearance that still requires source-based assessment before it can be treated as an established identity.

### Restricted appearance
A person appearance that may only appear publicly as a short crop or reading, because its birth year is after 1920 or cannot be placed at 1920 or earlier. Its counterpart is an open appearance.
