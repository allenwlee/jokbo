# The physical book settled what no scan or model could

Appendix item: 34. Dates: 21–23 November 2026 (JST).

## Summary

Several readings stayed open after every model pass and every scan comparison. The maintainer then checked the printed book itself.

- **A title character.** In the title 將仕郞, the last character's exact form was unclear on the scan. The maintainer selected 郞 (U+90DE) and asked for an editorial footnote on the 郎/郞 variant.
- **A gate character.** One name contained a character inside 門 (the "gate" component) that the models read as 閑 or 關. A comparison sheet of the same component from other pages had been taken to favor 閑. Looking at the sheet with the book in hand, the maintainer identified four examples as 閏 and one as 閑. The disputed example looked like 門 enclosing 東, matching none of them. The maintainer selected 闌, with a footnote recording the alternatives.
- **A blurred final character in a name.** Qwen read 录 and Gemini read 秉. Eleven printed instances of 秉 elsewhere in the book did not match its strokes. The maintainer checked the printed book and read 彔.
- **An overprint.** For the blurred character discussed in the lesson on invented explanations, the maintainer saw what looks like 父 with another character printed over it, possibly a printing error. The other character and the cause remain unconfirmed, and the edition keeps its tentative reading with a note.
- **A Hangul syllable in the closing prose, p. 135.** The maintainer ruled out 늦. The preceding column ends in 느, and the sentence needs 느꼈으며 ("felt"). The printed initial looks more like ㅋ than the clear double stroke of ㄲ elsewhere. The edition uses 꼈, with a note that context supports it while the printed strokes remain unclear.

In a 23 November review, the maintainer also supplied readings for 16 of the 20 passages that a third, in-context reading had left open.

## Impact

These readings could not be settled from the scan. A model's confident answer would have put an unsupported character into the edition.

## How it was noticed

The characters were left flagged as uncertain after model passes and same-book comparisons. The uncertainty list, kept with each proof, is what sent them to the physical book.

## Root cause

A scan loses detail in small, blurred or overprinted characters. No model can read detail that is not in the image.

## Fix

Each reading the maintainer chose is recorded with its source of evidence: inspection of the physical book. The proof's editorial notes keep the alternatives and the remaining uncertainty. Where the scan itself is clearer than the models' reading, the scan wins too. For example, in one date two models both read 戊 where the scan plainly shows 戌, and the draft kept 戌.

## Lesson

Keep a running list of uncertain readings, and check them against the physical book when you can. Record that the evidence came from the paper, not the scan.

Model agreement is never the final authority. The printed page is.

If you cannot reach the physical book, leave the reading open and say so in a note.
