# Model explanations can be invented

Appendix item: 16. Date: 21 November 2026 (JST).

## Summary

One blurred character in a modern entry stayed unresolved after several readings. Two further readings of a wider crop disagreed on it. One proposed 炭 and the other 發. Both read the next character as 石.

Their explanations were unreliable. One described the parts of 炭 incorrectly and invented a supporting fact that the scan does not show. The other answer was cut off and misread neighboring text.

## Impact

A confident explanation can make a guess look like evidence. If accepted, the invented fact could have been written into the edition or the database as if the book said it.

## How it was noticed

The explanations were checked against the crop and against what the visible strokes actually show. The component description did not match the character.

## Root cause

Asked to justify a reading, a model produces a plausible account whether or not the image supports it. Its explanation is generated text, not an observation.

## Fix

Neither proposal was applied. The raw responses and the crop were kept. The proof kept its earlier tentative reading, flagged as uncertain. The question was later checked against the physical book (see the lesson on the physical book).

## Lesson

Use a model's reading as a candidate. Do not treat its explanation as evidence. Check every claim it makes against the visible scan and the book's own cross-references.
